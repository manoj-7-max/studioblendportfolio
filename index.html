FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before working with StudioBlend.

How quickly can you start my project?+
I can typically start within 1–3 business days of receiving your brief and initial payment. For urgent projects, same-day starts are often possible — just let me know on WhatsApp.
Do you work with small businesses and startups?+
Absolutely! Most of my clients are small businesses, local brands, and early-stage startups in Chennai and Tamil Nadu. My pricing is built with freelancer rates — no agency overhead — so it's always affordable.
What is included in the free consultation?+
The free consultation is a 20–30 minute call or WhatsApp chat where we discuss your brand, goals, target audience, and what services would best suit you. No commitment required.
How do I pay? Do you require an advance?+
I accept UPI, bank transfer, and Razorpay. For monthly retainers, 100% advance is required. For project-based work, it's 50% upfront and 50% on delivery.
Can I request revisions?+
Yes! All packages include 2 rounds of revisions. Additional revisions are charged at a small flat rate. I always aim to get it right the first time based on your brief.
Do you offer custom packages?+
Yes, absolutely. The listed pricing plans are starting points. If you need a mix of services or a one-time project, just reach out and I'll put together a custom quote within 24 hours.
